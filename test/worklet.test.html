recorder-worklet.js

An oscillator at 440 Hz is pushed through the real suno-recorder worklet for about a second and a half, exactly the way offscreen.js wires it: the tap into the worklet, a gain of 0 to keep the graph pulling it, chunks transferred back, and WavAccumulator turning them into a WAV. What comes out proves the tap keeps the context's own sample rate, keeps every frame, and keeps the tone intact.

Add ?upload=1 and the encoded WAV is posted to test/out/ so the shell can check it.
